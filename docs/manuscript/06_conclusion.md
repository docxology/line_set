# Conclusion {#sec:conclusion}

The historical eight-line 0.6.1 measurement remains evidence about its original captures, not the ten-instrument declaration. Current namespace findings require the newly generated complete pinned record. Public disclosure must withhold Violet, Copper, and Iron source identities after full admission and must not claim complete public source-pin proof.

Whether the instruments overlap is still human work. Copper's finite-unit arithmetic and Iron's scenario-target comparisons differ from current label-coverage evaluators, but complete typed extensions could preserve those distinctions. Their separate packaging is an architectural decision, not a completed empirical admission result or proof of consumer need (see [the scholarship section](#sec:scholarship)).

What the project earns beyond the reading is the shape of its own constraint. A package that checks other packages for shared tokens has to survive its own rule, or the rule is something it imposes rather than something it holds. Prefixing the reading statuses and then running the wrapper's entry through the wrapper's own collision check closes that gap cheaply, and it forced one real design decision: the check needs the siblings installed, so it cannot live in the offline battery, so an unattended run reports it as failing rather than passing. I would rather have a check that says *unestablished* on a bare machine than one that says *fine* because it had nothing to compare. Writing that condition down as [@prop:self-disjointness], with a test that re-derives it, is what keeps the sentence and the function from drifting apart.

This work adds no evaluator of its own. The substantive instruments retain their native jobs, now including Copper's resource exposure arithmetic and Iron's recorded recovery comparisons. Neither result compensates for a Red refusal or missing Violet consent. Main declares the set, reads available published vocabularies, and reports exact cross-line name collisions without a matching exemption. An appended declaration does not itself establish collision freedom, conceptual independence, or native correctness. Those boundaries and the required evidence refresh remain explicit in [the extensibility section](#sec:extensibility).

The work is built for DAF's public research index at [docxology](https://github.com/docxology/docxology) — machine-readable, cross-linked, and verification-logged — with its public home at `docxology/line_set`. The set's short orientation note, `docs/line-set.md`, remains an unpublished working note in the author's private projects tree and does not travel with this repository, so it is cited by name rather than linked; this paper is the version with a package under it and a reading attached.

# Abstract {#sec:abstract}

Ten small instruments are declared, each with a bounded question. Red records refusals; Black, disciplined work; Golden, aspiration; White, absence and restraint; Silver, memory and succession; Violet, affected parties' consent records; Blue, ongoing stewardship; Green, capacity under development. Copper adds dimensioned finite-resource exposure arithmetic and separate burden attribution. Iron adds scenario-bound comparisons of recorded recovery exercises with declared targets. Main adds no instrument of its own: it holds the declaration, reads available sibling packages, and checks published enum member names for unexempted cross-line collisions. Copper and Iron are separate packages by architectural choice, not because the historical empirical admission protocol was completed.

The package is pure standard library and depends on nothing. Its `LineEntry` tuple and exact shared-token exemptions are declared in `registry.py`. Its reader runs five recorded stages — resolve, bind, collide, declare, status — and returns one of four readings, in fixed precedence: `SET_COLLIDING`, then `SET_UNDECLARED`, then `SET_PARTIAL`, then `SET_LEGIBLE`. A live collision is never hidden behind a missing install. A package that cannot be imported yields no version, registry size, or digest rather than a fabricated value.

Eight structural checks run offline over the declaration alone; a ninth, which needs the siblings importable, applies this package's own collision check to a declaration that includes this package; it is the reason the four reading statuses are `SET_`-prefixed, and it is the property the project exists to hold. The objects, the five stages, the precedence rule, the exemption matcher's fail-closed conditions, and that self-application are stated as definitions or propositions. Behavioral tests exercise the implementation; reviewing the authored statements against it remains a separate task.

`SET_LEGIBLE` means one thing and I want it stated before anything else: every declared vocabulary was readable and no unexempted published-name collision was found. It is not a finding that the instruments are correct, complete, good, sufficient, or worth having, and it is not a finding about behavior. The reader reads names.

The preserved eight-line 0.6.1 measurement is historical evidence, not a census of the expanded declaration. New counts and collision findings require a complete actual pinned reading of all declared producers; this source integration predicts none. The public view must withhold Violet, Copper, and Iron source identities after full admission, while the complete pinned record stays private. Disjoint spellings do not establish disjoint concepts.

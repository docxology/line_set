records their body identity and decisions, not their private inbox paths or
attachments. The pinned source references in [handoffs](sibling-requirements.md)
locate the source traces. No new external scholarship or empirical result is
introduced. A future dossier must retain its own consented source custody and
redaction decisions. Public copies must not include restricted raw evidence.

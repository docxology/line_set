# Copper and Iron: architectural admission, 2026-11-08

This decision adds Copper at working position 9 and Iron at position 10, both
without an opus stage. The authoritative entries are in
[`registry.py`](../../src/line_set/registry.py). Witness remains a separate
companion, not a declaration entry. The current family has ten substantive
instruments, the colorless Main reader, and Witness: twelve projects. An
authorized private omnibus contains eleven works, excluding Witness.

## Decision and evidence class

The owner requests Copper and delegates selection of one or a few other elements;
Iron is the selected companion. This is an architectural package-placement
decision, not a finding that the historical empirical admission protocol passed.
No reader study, observed demand, executed matched-case comparison against the
extension alternatives, or superiority finding is claimed. Native runtime,
package, and namespace qualification is separate from those empirical questions.
The earlier dated [no-addition decision](README.md), [dossier](dossier.md),
[protocol](protocol.md), and [2026-11-06 refinement](../refinement-20261106.md)
retain their original scope; their empirical requirements have not been
retroactively satisfied. Claim-evidence audit distinguishes source-level
computation, exercised native behavior, and still-unobserved practical usefulness.

## Copper: finite-unit arithmetic, not coverage or approval

Black can retain a budget document and its method/evidence labels. Blue can retain
an obligation and its care signals. Witness can retain an allocation statement,
a human calculation, disagreement, and a return. Those are the strongest existing
representations; none of their current evaluators computes finite-pool exposure.
A typed resource attachment to Blue or Black could implement the same arithmetic.
Separate packaging keeps their existing coverage schemas and outputs focused; it
is not proof that such an extension loses information.

Copper owns explicit pool/resource/unit/exact-period boundaries, nonnegative
integer measured or estimated bands, and null quantities. Recorded use plus
*remaining* commitments produces per-pool lower/upper exposure comparisons;
proposals stay separate. For capacity 8, use 2 plus outstanding 5 is exposure 7,
not 5 and not 9. Changing only the commitment to 7 changes that comparison to
exceeded without changing Black's budget-document coverage or Blue's care labels.
These are synthetic distinguishing inputs, not observed allocations or evidence of
consumer need. No cross-unit sum or fairness score is meaningful. Unknown exposure blocks a within-limit
conclusion, but cannot erase a known lower-bound exceedance. Unknown proposals
do not make committed exposure incomplete; proposal-only pools have no exposure
entries, not an allocation success. Malformed dimensions and duplicate identities
cannot be discarded to improve the arithmetic.

Attribution, withholding, contestation, and declared authorization references
remain separate from amounts. A contested charge is not a discount; attributed
burden is not liability, acceptance, consent, or spending authority. Copper cannot
verify that independent caller-authored snapshots preserve all historical use or
that an outstanding amount is truthful. It does not perform cancellation,
conservation checking across revisions, or gross-to-net conversion.

## Iron: scenario-target comparisons, not rehearsal-label coverage

Silver already records succession rehearsal and custody; Blue records working
verification and continuing care. Witness can preserve a recovery obligation and
an authored conclusion, but deliberately does not evaluate its inequality.
Current Silver/Blue label coverage is unchanged when a recorded restoration takes
2400 rather than 1200 seconds against a declared 1800-second target. Iron's
identity-bound comparison must distinguish those inputs. That is a different
computation, not evidence that a drill occurred or future recovery is assured.

The strongest alternative is a complete typed Silver rehearsal attachment or Blue
verification attachment carrying scenario identity, targets, metrics, required
outcomes, dates, all episodes, and canonical replay context. It can preserve every
proposed Iron distinction while leaving custody/care projections intact. We do
not claim to have defeated that alternative. Iron is selected to keep disruption
comparison policy, versioning, replay, and native status independently focused,
not because separate-instrument necessity or consumer demand was established.

Iron binds each exercise to the complete scenario digest (including targets),
procedure digest, environment, and date. Missing metrics are not zero; mismatched
or future episodes cannot support the current scenario. A failed required outcome
or exceeded supplied limit remains missed alongside missing/aged flags. Every
episode remains visible, including contradictory or unfavorable runs. There is
no favorable scenario-wide READY/RESILIENT aggregate, best-run selection, uptime
warranty, or inference that referenced observations are true.

## Integration, privacy, and measured evidence

Main continues to read versions, registry identities, and actual published root
enum names, never native evaluations. Prefixing Copper/Iron vocabularies supplies
no conceptual admission proof and requires no predicted exemption. The existing
exact shared-token matcher and reader remain generic. Actual collision/census
results belong to the [dated pinned measurement](../manuscript/04_examples.md),
not to this architectural decision.

Violet, Copper, and Iron are PRIVATE. Their repository names are access-aware
references, not promises of public source. Public Main must withhold all three
source revisions and module paths only after complete real pinned admission.
Full native provenance, private measurement, and the eleven-work omnibus remain
in separate authorized private custody. Public Witness exports require separate
disclosure review and empty source snapshot references; Main does not generate
them or reinterpret their native statuses. Neither arithmetic nor successful
exercise comparisons compensate for a Red refusal or missing Violet consent.

The actual dated ten-source reading, generated native ledgers and figures, and
matching/full-public refusal checks are recorded in the
[release packet](../releases/v0.7.0.md). The preserved eight-line evidence remains
historical; it is not relabeled as that new measurement. These computations
establish the stated namespace and custody properties, not empirical necessity.

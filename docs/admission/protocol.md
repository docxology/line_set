# Bounded comparison protocol (not executed)

Revision: 2026-10-21. Owner of the admission decision: Daniel Ari Friedman.
This is a feasibility design, not a powered population study or a finding.
The [library](cases.json) contains hypothetical prompts only, never allegations
about actual people. All representation and reviewer-result slots start empty.

## Preconditions and consent

First obtain owner receipts for the [native requirements](sibling-requirements.md),
including scoped resolution, exact return identity and structural admission in
Witness. Record source revisions and unresolved defects. Do not treat a release
tag alone as proof that a requirement was implemented. Do not run a substantive
comparison on a substrate known to confuse classification with resolution.

Use the proposed finite corpus: six likely repair cases, six existing-family
controls and six ambiguous/overlapping cases. Daniel may later supply up to six
voluntary, redacted historical examples with explicit use consent. A missing
historical sample leaves observed demand unestablished. Never manufacture it.
The allocation hypothesis remains deferred even where an ambiguous prompt
mentions a burden.

## Materials and independent review

For every case, retain its question, source class, scope, strongest existing
family representation, exact native outputs, Witness relations/returns, proposed
missing distinction, minimal extension representation, separate repair-record
representation, evidence/replay references and nonclaims. The library's empty
arrays mean not supplied, never successful execution. Preserve outputs as bytes
or exact files with their native schema identity; no cross-line translation.

Ask two reviewers independently for one of: existing coverage, native extension,
infrastructure, potentially new instrument, unresolved. Retain both reasons,
source revisions and disagreements without majority-vote settlement. Owners
must fairly assess nearest-neighbor extensions, not only a weak baseline.

**First stop condition:** if all material distinctions fit existing records or
small native extensions, publish a reasoned **do not add** memo and stop
expansion. An unresolved representation is not evidence of a gap by itself.

For remaining gaps compare three plain-text arms, without colour names in the
reader-facing materials: current composition; composition with targeted native
extensions; genuinely separate repair record. Keep the researcher's native
provenance map outside the blinded materials. Counterbalance presentation order
and record it. Match the indicated cases while holding native reports fixed;
for converse controls, change one native fact while holding repair facts fixed.
No assertion of independence is made before actual outputs exist.

Ask: who made the statement; what is unknown; has anyone authorized action;
can one party close another's concern; what exactly returned; which boundary
remains; where is the evidence? Record case-level correct/incorrect/unresolved
interpretations with a prewritten answer rationale, false permission inferences,
lost objections, disclosure pressure, explanation burden and ambiguity. Keep
category findings separate. Optional descriptive time must not compensate for a
boundary violation. Do not compute a composite usefulness or fairness score.

A proposed pilot uses six to eight voluntary readers. Record selection,
familiarity, consent, withdrawals, redactions and exact materials. A smaller
pilot is formative only and must be labelled. No recruitment, collection or
study execution is authorized by this scaffold. All results remain absent.

## Explicit decision paths

Any silence-as-agreement, compensation-as-clearance or unearned closure finding
blocks admission pending redesign. Zero observed violations would be necessary
pilot evidence, not a zero-error guarantee. Publish negative results and dissent.
Possible outcomes are do not add, extend a native instrument, improve transport,
request more evidence, or justify a bounded independent candidate. No palette
argument, scalar score or lexical pass can justify admission.

Only after human justification should a candidate acquire its own registry,
native schema, claim ledger, tests, manuscript, maintenance owner and compatibility
plan. Only then consider a unique identifier and `opus_stage=None` under the
existing extension recipe. None is created in this release.

If eventually adopted, agree an observation endpoint before use: the first ten
voluntary cases or six weeks of intended use, whichever supplies that agreed
endpoint. This is a proposed window, not a monitoring service. Review duplication,
ambiguity, coercive closure pressure and permission confusion. No real cases
means demand remains unestablished. Retain narrowly, revise, fold into an
extension or retire while preserving old record interpretation. Filled fields
and fewer unresolved cases are not success measures.

## Conceptual-overlap checklist

For each nearest neighbor retain its question, strongest representation and
native outputs verbatim, proposed minimal extension, extension-owner response,
and the precise distinction still lost. Include Witness relation and return
records rather than assuming they are insufficient. Preserve an owner's
disagreement. A new interface or clearer prose alone is not an ontology gap.

Also check Black practice, Golden direction, Silver custody and Green capacity
where the case touches them. The wrapper has no completeness wheel to fill.

## Negative conceptual example: the renamed Black clone

Suppose a proposer copies Black's question, object and evaluation relation but
renames every exported member with a fresh prefix. Even a clean lexical reading
would say nothing about conceptual independence. Reject this proposal because
the extension rebuttal supplies no lost distinction and the independent question
is identical. This is a human counterexample, not an executed sibling clone,
new registry entry, or new token exemption. Lexical success cannot admit it.

## Proposed repair record, only if comparison justifies it

Use a private/plain-text prototype, not a package: stable issue and bounded
subject references; attributed concern with evidence class; affected-party and
respondent references with representation limits; separately attributed request
and remedy; dated events/evidence references; declared standing and standing
disputes; partial delivery, receipt, disputed adequacy, request withdrawal and
unrecorded response as separate facts; protected references and native reports.
Do not introduce a universal resolved state. Reported delivery, acknowledged
receipt, disputed adequacy and an open remainder can coexist.

It must not become a court, guilt classifier, damages calculator, forgiveness
ledger, complaint ranking, coerced reconciliation or permission to repeat a
prohibited act. A structurally valid record must allow protected details to
remain undisclosed and a remedy to remain unaccepted.

## Required adversarial review constraints

1. A respondent's completion claim cannot manufacture affected-party acceptance.
2. Silence remains silence, never satisfaction, forgiveness, waiver or consent.
3. One party's acceptance cannot settle another party's concern.
4. Partial repair preserves history and the open remainder.
5. Legitimate request withdrawal does not establish that the account was false.
6. A Red boundary remains blocking regardless of repair or favorable reports.
7. Reopening requires an explicit rule; duplicate events cannot erase history.
8. Standing disputes remain visible regardless of submission order.
9. Shared envelopes need not copy restricted material.
10. Unresolved is an allowed terminal review outcome.

Record each constraint by case, exact material inspected, reviewer, result,
reason and limitation. These are requirements for a future prototype, not test
results or a proof of justice. Narrow or reject a prototype that requires
substantive adjudication to preserve them.

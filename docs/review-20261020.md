## Architectural decisions

and deliberate empty-exemption refusal are broader than minimal custom-set
admission. Both paths share minimal identity and field validation instead.

I retained namespace status when optional registry metadata or discovery evidence
is incomplete. The separate `check_set.py --require-pinned --as-of ...` lane
requires pinned sources, complete package metadata and completed discovery.
Neither lane evaluates siblings or establishes conceptual separation.

sibling source was edited. Violet's review limitation remains historical until
the pinned release reading records actual source access. The prior reading is

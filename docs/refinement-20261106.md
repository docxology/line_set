identity checks, lookup, or projection. It still preserves native reports,

mandatory family dependency is added.

## Additional-color hypotheses — not admitted

**Amber: contestation and repair.** What remains contested or owed, who can
challenge closure, and what repair has actually been recorded? It must not
become a court, forgiveness mechanism, or permission to cross a Red refusal.
First compare a concrete dispute against Blue stewardship, Violet responses,
White remainders, and a Witness decision record. Admit it only if that
comparison reveals a distinct native question and nonredundant behavior.

**Copper: resources and burdens.** What time, money, compute, or access is
committed, who bears the cost, and which allocations remain contested? Keep
measured, estimated, authorized, and disputed quantities separate. It must not
price consent, dignity, or refusal, or turn resource optimization into an
override. First try the same case as a Witness allocation record and existing
Black/Blue evidence before proposing another instrument.

Neither hypothesis has a registry entry, package, native status vocabulary,
claimed implementation, or admission decision. Teal remains an isolated
extension fixture, not a proposal or an admitted ninth color.
